Start with a question. Answer with Books connects original passages from different books into a coherent reading, with enough context to enter each scene and understand how the experiences relate.

  <Card title="Install the skill" href="/guide/quickstart-skill">Use the same reading experience through your agent.</Card>
  <Card title="Add a book" href="/guide/upload">Upload a PDF or EPUB for free hosted processing.</Card>
</CardGroup>

The shared library and completed guides are public. The $24/month subscription includes 30 new guides per billing month and source-text access through the skill. Original files stay private and are not offered as downloads.

Ask a question, confirm the suggested books, and choose about 10, 25, or 45 minutes. The model reads source passages, prepares the context, connects the scenes and checks the finished guide against the original text. See [your plan](/guide/billing) and [the shared library](/guide/library).
