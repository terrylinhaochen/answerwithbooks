---
title: "Books and guides"
description: "A shared library organized around questions."
---
Books contains the existing editorial shelf and newly processed shared books. A book's page introduces its author and subject, offers a short summary, then shows three **Questions to explore with this book** with more available to expand.

Guides begins with a question. The model suggests books, you confirm them and a reading time, and a processing card becomes a continuous reading of original passages with context and a closing synthesis. Finished guides are public; Your Guides keeps your creation history. Reading progress and feedback belong to your account.

New uploads join the public library. Old private books and guides remain private unless explicitly published; changing the product does not change their visibility. Removing a shared book from your shelf leaves its source available to everyone else.
