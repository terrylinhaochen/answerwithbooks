Once connected, ask your agent:

```text
Use my books to help me divide responsibilities across a growing team.
```

The agent searches relevant chapter methods, reads the evidence, and applies useful ideas to your question. The agent you are using writes the answer; saved book skills are reusable instructions and references.

## One skill is enough

Keep the generic Answer with Books skill for a large library. You do not need to clone a repository or install every book. Individual book skills are optional for favorites and local use.

To install one, ask “Install the skill for this book.” A ready full-source package includes `SKILL.md`, chapter notes, glossary, patterns, cheatsheet, and source references. Some hosts discover newly installed skills on the next turn or in a new session.

## Hosted retrieval or local installation

Hosted retrieval is the default for a connected private library. Your agent asks the authenticated `book-library` endpoint for relevant methods and source excerpts, then applies that evidence to your task. The endpoint does not execute `SKILL.md` or write the final answer. You do not need to download each book.

The reader page uses **Copy prompt for your AI**. It includes the editorial digest and task instructions; a connected agent can retrieve the full cited methods when needed. The **Agent Skill Card** is collapsed under **Put this book to work**. Optional CLI exports and offline installation remain available, but no download is required for hosted use.

Own-agent generation saves work locally and can sync a newly completed AWB workspace to your private library; see [Generate with your own agent](/guide/own-agent) for availability and limits. You can also [combine sources and update a guide](/guide/combined-guides).

## Read and manage your books

Private reader pages follow the same structure as the public shelf: when to reach for the book, what it is about, an editorial summary, an Amazon link, and **Put this book to work**. Core lessons and frameworks are optional expandable reference notes. An excerpt remains labeled with its scope. Amazon links for uploads search by title and author; they do not imply a verified edition.

Open [Manage Skills](https://answerwithbooks.com/manage-skills/) from your profile or book page:

- **Archive** hides a book from your active shelf and excludes its skill from hosted retrieval. Restore it from the Archived tab.
- **Delete** permanently removes the book, its skill, uploaded sources and saved revisions after confirmation. Billing records required for your account are retained.
- Pause active work and let its current step finish before managing it. Settle or cancel an active paid conversion first. Archiving does not cancel a charge or remove files installed on your device.

[Books in the making](https://answerwithbooks.com/processing/) is the full-width queue for unfinished conversions. Completed reader pages do not show source replacement or download controls. Agent/CLI guide updates remain available through [combined guides](/guide/combined-guides).

Earlier conversions may contain only skill notes. They show an explicit missing-summary state rather than presenting those notes as a finished editorial digest. New managed conversions and CLI 0.7.0 local generations produce both outputs.

## Public and private sources

Public recommendations can include books outside the catalog. When choosing a reading set, the agent should show the selection criteria and let you choose before starting a conversion.

Private search only uses your connected account's eligible books. Hosted semantic retrieval sends the search question to the configured embedding provider. Public CLI retrieval uses the bundled public corpus.

## Disconnect

Ask your agent to log out of Answer with Books. This revokes its account connection. Book skills already installed on your machine remain local files after logout.
