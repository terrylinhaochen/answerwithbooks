title: "Your subscription"
description: "$24 per month for 30 new guides."
---
Every signed-in account gets **3 free guides per calendar month**, resetting on the first at 00:00 UTC. No payment card is required. Failed generations do not consume the allowance; deleting a completed guide does not restore it.

The paid reading plan costs **$24/month** and includes **30 new guides per billing month** and source-text access through the skill. Uploading books and reading published books and guides are free.

A guide allowance is reserved when you confirm the suggested books and reading time. Failed generation returns its allowance. Retries, reopening a guide, and asking questions about existing guides do not use another allowance. Removing a completed guide does not refund it. Unused allowances do not roll over, and there are no automatic overage charges.

[Billing](https://answerwithbooks.com/billing/) shows remaining guides and the renewal date. Stripe confirms payment before access starts. Returning from checkout alone does not activate a subscription. If checkout is unavailable, the service says so and does not charge you.

Use **Manage subscription** to manage payment details and cancellation. Published guides remain readable after a subscription ends. Earlier prepaid balances, reservations and receipts are preserved under **Previous billing**; they are not silently converted into subscription payments.
