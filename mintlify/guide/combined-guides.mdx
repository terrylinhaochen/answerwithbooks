---
title: "Create a guide"
description: "Connect original passages around your question."
---
Open [Guides](https://answerwithbooks.com/guides/) and ask your question. The model suggests books from the shared library. Confirm the selection and choose about 10, 25, or 45 minutes. Your guide appears as a processing card, then opens as one continuous reading.

You can do the same through the Answer with Books skill. Combining uploaded files into a synthetic book has been retired; guides connect passages from distinct editions while preserving each source.
