---
title: "Existing private books"
description: "Access to books saved before the shared library."
---

Existing private books remain private and readable by their owner. Their receipts and prepaid balances are preserved. Open them from your profile; removing a shared book from your shelf does not delete its public source.

For all new books and guides, use the [Reading API](/api/reading) and [hosted upload flow](/guide/upload). New shared uploads are free; the previous quote, local-generation, and per-book skill workflow is retired from the product.
